Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information Cognita collects, how we use it, when it may be shared, and the choices and rights available to you. We aim to collect only the information necessary to provide, secure, and improve the Service. We do not sell your personal information or use your prompts, uploads, or generated content to train AI models.

1. Who We Are

Cognita ("Cognita," "we," "us," or "our") is a service created and operated by Veritone Services, a business registered in Nigeria with the Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC) under Registration Number BN: 8940276.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you use the Cognita website and application (the "Service").

For questions about this Privacy Policy, your personal information, or how your information is handled, you can contact us at info@cognita.com.ng.

2. Information We Collect

Account information. When you create an account, we collect information needed to create and manage your account, such as your email address. If you sign in using Google, Facebook, or another supported authentication provider, we may receive basic profile information made available by that provider, such as your name, email address, and profile photo. We do not receive or store your authentication provider's password.

Connected social accounts. If you choose to connect a Facebook Page or an Instagram account to Cognita's social features, we receive an access token issued by Meta and the identifiers needed to recognize that Page or account (such as the Page ID or Instagram user ID). We use this only to provide the social features you have enabled, such as publishing content, reading messages or comments, or responding on your behalf. You can disconnect a linked Page or account at any time.

Connected Google account data. If you choose to connect your Google Account to Cognita's Google connector, we receive an OAuth access token and refresh token from Google, scoped only to the specific permissions ("scopes") you grant during the Google consent screen. Cognita currently requests only the following scopes: Google Calendar events on calendars you can see (read and write, including listing your calendars); Google Drive access limited to files that Cognita itself creates (Cognita cannot browse, search, or read pre-existing files in your Drive); and Gmail access limited to sending email on your behalf and creating, listing, or renaming Gmail labels (Cognita cannot read, search, or open your existing emails or drafts). We use this access only to provide the Google-connected features you have enabled, such as showing or managing your calendar, saving a file Cognita created for you, or sending an email you asked Cognita to send. Our access to, and use of, information received through Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements: we do not use Google user data to serve advertising, we do not sell or transfer Google user data except as necessary to provide the features described here, for security purposes, or to comply with law, and we do not allow humans to read your Google data except with your affirmative agreement, to investigate a security issue or abuse, or to comply with applicable law. You can disconnect your Google Account from Cognita at any time; see Section 12 below.

Connected GitHub account data. If you choose to connect your GitHub account to Cognita, we receive an OAuth access token scoped to GitHub's repo permission, which allows read and write access to both your public and private repositories. We use this access only to carry out the specific GitHub actions you request through Cognita, such as reading or writing files in a repository you choose to work with; we do not browse your repositories other than as needed to complete a request you make. You can disconnect GitHub from Cognita at any time; see Section 12 below.

Connected Canva account data. If you choose to connect your Canva account to Cognita, we receive an OAuth access token scoped to reading and writing your Canva designs, folders, and assets, and reading your Canva profile information. We use this access only to carry out the specific Canva actions you request through Cognita, such as creating or editing a design or uploading an asset. You can disconnect Canva from Cognita at any time; see Section 12 below.

Content you provide. When you use Cognita, you may provide text prompts, uploaded files, images, documents, social media posts or messages you choose to send through the Service, and other information that you choose to submit to the Service ("Input"). We may also store responses generated by the Service ("Output") when necessary to provide features such as conversation history, saved conversations, or other functionality you choose to use.

AI Inbox and AI Insights Digest. If you use Cognita's AI Inbox feature, we store the Facebook and Instagram comments and direct messages associated with your connected Page or Instagram account so that Cognita can classify them and draft suggested replies. Replies are only sent when you choose to send them; Cognita does not send replies automatically. If you use the AI Insights Digest feature, we pull Page and Instagram account insights (such as reach and engagement metrics) through your Facebook connection, use them to generate a plain-language summary and a PDF digest, and store that PDF so it can be emailed to you or shared by you through a WhatsApp link that you choose to send yourself.

Usage information. We collect information about how you use the Service, such as the features you use, the number and type of requests you make, and usage associated with your subscription or plan. This information helps us provide the Service, enforce usage limits, maintain security, and understand how the Service is being used.

Payment information. Payments are processed by Paystack. We do not collect or store your full payment card details. Payment information is handled by Paystack in accordance with its own privacy and security practices. We may receive information such as confirmation of a successful or failed transaction, a transaction reference, and information about the plan or subscription associated with the payment.

Technical information. We may automatically collect standard technical information when you use the Service, including your IP address, browser type, device type, operating system, approximate location derived from technical information, and information about how your browser or device interacts with the Service. We use this information for security, fraud prevention, troubleshooting, diagnostics, and service improvement.

Communications. If you contact us for support or otherwise communicate with us, we may retain the information you provide, including your email address and the contents of your communication, so that we can respond to you and keep appropriate records.

3. How We Use Your Information

We may use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, operate, maintain, and improve the Service;
  • Create and manage your account;
  • Authenticate users and protect accounts from unauthorized access;
  • Connect, manage, and disconnect your Facebook Pages and Instagram accounts when you choose to use Cognita's social features;
  • Connect, manage, and disconnect your Google Account, and carry out the specific Google Calendar, Google Drive, or Gmail actions you request, when you choose to use Cognita's Google connector;
  • Connect, manage, and disconnect your GitHub account, and carry out the specific repository actions you request, when you choose to use Cognita's GitHub connector;
  • Connect, manage, and disconnect your Canva account, and carry out the specific design actions you request, when you choose to use Cognita's Canva connector;
  • Classify and draft suggested replies to incoming Facebook and Instagram comments and messages, and generate performance digests from your Page and Instagram insights, when you choose to use Cognita's AI Inbox or AI Insights Digest features;
  • Process payments and manage subscriptions;
  • Enforce plan features, entitlements, and usage limits;
  • Process your requests and generate AI responses;
  • Provide conversation history and other features you choose to use;
  • Respond to questions and support requests;
  • Detect, prevent, and investigate fraud, abuse, security incidents, and other misuse;
  • Monitor and improve the performance, reliability, and safety of the Service;
  • Communicate with you about your account, transactions, service updates, and important notices;
  • Comply with applicable laws, regulations, legal processes, or lawful requests.

We do not sell your personal information. We also do not use your Input or Output to train our own AI models or intentionally provide it to third parties for the purpose of training their general AI models.

4. How Cognita Processes Your AI Requests

When you submit a prompt, upload a file, or otherwise make an AI request through Cognita, your Input is processed by our backend infrastructure and may be sent to third-party AI infrastructure providers that we use to generate the requested response.

These providers may process your Input and related technical information on our behalf for the purpose of providing the AI functionality. We select providers based on factors such as service quality, security, reliability, and applicable data-processing terms. The providers we use may change over time as Cognita develops.

We do not use your Input or Output to train our own AI models. We also take reasonable steps to ensure that third-party AI providers used by Cognita process submitted content in accordance with the applicable terms governing their services.

You should avoid submitting highly sensitive personal information or information that you are not authorized to share. You are responsible for ensuring that your use of the Service and the information you submit complies with applicable laws and your own obligations to third parties.

8. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, including providing the Service, maintaining account records, resolving disputes, enforcing agreements, preventing fraud and abuse, and complying with legal, tax, accounting, or regulatory obligations.

Account information is generally retained while your account remains active. If you use features that save conversations or other content, that information may be retained until you delete it, delete your account, or it is otherwise no longer required for the relevant Service feature.

Facebook/Instagram access tokens and connected-account identifiers are retained only while the connection is active and are deleted when you disconnect, delete your account, or when Meta notifies us of a deletion request.

Google OAuth access tokens and refresh tokens are retained only while your Google connection is active and are deleted when you disconnect your Google Account or delete your Cognita account. Any calendar events, Gmail messages, or Drive files created through the connector live in your own Google Account, governed by Google's own retention practices, not ours; Cognita does not keep a separate copy of that content beyond what is briefly needed to complete the requested action.

GitHub and Canva OAuth access (and, where issued, refresh) tokens are retained only while the respective connection is active and are deleted when you disconnect that connector or delete your Cognita account.

Facebook/Instagram comments and messages stored for the AI Inbox feature, and digests generated by the AI Insights Digest feature, are retained until you delete them, disconnect the associated Facebook connection, or delete your Cognita account.

You can delete your Cognita account at any time from within the Service under Account → Delete account, which immediately and permanently deletes the information described in this section, subject to what we are legally required or otherwise permitted to retain. You do not need to email us to do this, though you are welcome to.

Certain information may need to be retained after account deletion where required by law, necessary to resolve disputes, prevent fraud or abuse, enforce our agreements, or protect our legitimate interests.

However, no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is completely secure. While we work to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Facebook and Instagram Data Deletion

If you sign in to Cognita using "Continue with Facebook," or connect a Facebook Page or Instagram account to Cognita's social features, you can ask us to delete the information we received from Facebook in two ways:

  • In Cognita, go to Account → Connected apps and disconnect Facebook, or delete your Cognita account entirely; or
  • In your own Facebook settings, remove "Cognita" from Settings → Apps and Websites. Facebook then automatically notifies us to delete the corresponding data on our side, with no further action needed from you.

Either path deletes the same thing: any Facebook/Instagram access token, Page or Instagram account identifiers, and the internal record we use to recognize your Facebook account on future sign-ins. We do not otherwise retain a copy of your Facebook profile, Page content, or Instagram content beyond what a given feature needs while your connection is active.

When Facebook notifies us of a deletion request, we process it immediately and give you a confirmation code and a status page you can check at app.cognita.com.ng/data-deletion-status.html. You can also always email info@cognita.com.ng to request this directly.

12. Connector Account Connection and Disconnection

If you connect your Google Account to Cognita's Google connector, you can revoke Cognita's access at any time in two ways:

  • In Cognita, go to Account → Connected apps and disconnect Google, or delete your Cognita account entirely; or
  • In your Google Account settings, go to Security → Third-party apps with account access and remove Cognita's access directly with Google.

Either path immediately revokes the associated Google OAuth access and refresh tokens. Disconnecting through your Google Account settings does not automatically notify Cognita, so we also recommend disconnecting Google within Cognita, or emailing info@cognita.com.ng, so we can promptly delete the corresponding token and connection record on our side. We do not otherwise retain a copy of your Google Calendar, Drive, or Gmail content beyond what a given feature needs while your connection is active, consistent with the Limited Use requirements described in Section 2.

If you connect your GitHub account or your Canva account, the same two-path pattern applies: disconnect it in Cognita under Account → Connected apps (or delete your Cognita account entirely), or remove Cognita's access directly in that provider's own third-party access settings. Disconnecting directly with GitHub or Canva does not automatically notify Cognita, so we recommend also disconnecting within Cognita, or emailing info@cognita.com.ng, so we can promptly delete the corresponding token on our side.
